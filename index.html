Raw audio signals — tempo, energy, valence — have near-zero predictive power on Spotify. Artist reputation explains 35× more variance. The music isn't the message; the name behind it is.

Accuracy Hides a Diversity Collapse

A matrix factorization model scored NDCG@10 of 0.95 on 25M ratings — and covered less than 0.1% of the catalog. The model was technically excellent and behaviorally broken.

Getting People In Is Not the Same as Keeping Them

Stamp-game users on the LUCAUS festival platform engaged 3.5× more than average — then left the moment the game ended. People drawn in by one hook need a reason to stay. Without a next action, even your most engaged users vanish.

Core Research Thesis
"When predictions fail — why?"

Most recommendation systems optimize for accuracy. My work focuses on the outcomes that accuracy can't explain — surprise hits, hidden gems, and structural biases baked into data before any model sees it. Fame explains only a fraction of variance; the rest is where the interesting behavioral questions live.

04 About

Operating at the intersection of Behavior, Data, and People.

I’m not interested in technology for its own sake. I’m fascinated by how humans perceive, trust, and change their behavior when interacting with algorithms — and by moments when systems fail people quietly, without any metric noticing.

My triple major in Psychology, English Literature, and Technology-Art isn't a detour. It's the reason I can read both a residual plot and the human story behind it. I study systems that fail users without failing metrics — and want to build ones that don’t.

I’m drawn to UX Research, Behavioral Data Science, and Human-AI Interaction roles where explaining why users behave the way they do matters more than maximizing model accuracy. I’m currently preparing MS applications with music recommendation systems as my core research portfolio.
